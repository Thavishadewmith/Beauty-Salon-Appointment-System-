About Liyo Salon

Our Mission

To empower every client to feel confident and beautiful by providing exceptional hair and beauty services in a welcoming, modern, and inclusive environment. We strive to set trends, deliver excellence, and inspire self-expression through our artistry and care.

Our Vision

To be the leading unisex salon in Sri Lanka, recognized for innovation, creativity, and a relentless commitment to customer satisfaction. We envision a community where everyone celebrates their unique beauty and style.

Our Commitment

We are dedicated to continuous learning, using the highest quality products, and maintaining the highest standards of hygiene and professionalism. Our team is committed to making every visit a memorable and uplifting experience for you.

Our Journey

Founded in the heart of Nugegoda, Liyo Salon began as a dream to redefine beauty care. Over the years, we have grown into a trusted destination for clients seeking style, relaxation, and transformation. Our journey is marked by passion, creativity, and the smiles of those we serve.
